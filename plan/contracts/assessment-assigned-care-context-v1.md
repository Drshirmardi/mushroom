# Assessment context for explicitly authorized assigned care v1 — FROZEN

Phase12/F12-PJ-D01, ADR-0028. Depends on accepted P02/C04/O02/G01-G02 and existing booking/clinical/text-consultation contracts. Continuous owner authorization includes connecting patient results to authorized professional consultations. This additive technical contract grants no default clinical capability or legal consent, changes no frozen clinical/chat/appointment contract and performs no automated clinical review.

Scope: one patient's explicit permission to share one owned COMPLETE noninvalidated individual assessment summary with the exact assigned qualified physician of one existing confirmed appointment, ONLINE or IN_PERSON. A coupled beneficiary may share its separately owned individual result; this grants no access to joint education, the partner, any raw response or score. Existing admin/raw assessment routes stay unchanged. No implied permission from payment, booking, medical processing consent or account linking.

One retained AssessmentCareShare ledger is justified: per-appointment/source/physician consent and final withdrawal cannot be derived from global processing consent. Pin patient/clinic, appointment/assigned doctor, exact source submission and technical source hash, original current O02 protocol review/hash, own exact unwithdrawn processing consent, owner-controlled ASSESSMENT_CARE_SHARING policy/version/hash, canonical context hash, independent accepting session/actor/time/key and final withdrawing session/actor/time/key. No response, score, individual band or clinical text duplicated into the ledger/audit. Immutable except final own withdrawal; one active grant per appointment/source, idempotent patient-scoped keys, retained history and no rebind/revival. New explicit grant after withdrawal is allowed. Retention/deletion remains an actual legal/operator release decision, not invented unrestricted retention.

Use existing ConsentPolicyVersion and explicit release CLI opt-in for ASSESSMENT_CARE_SHARING; no policy body or real clinical question/content seeded. Preview requires current exact O02 approval, supported non-review-required persisted technical provenance, active purpose/hash/locale-specific care policy, own current source access and a qualified assigned clinician. Existing P02 processing consent endpoint supplies the own required processing record; a paid source must use its original bound processing record, a legacy source requires an explicit current exact processing acceptance. Care acceptance is scoped evidence on this ledger, analogous to C03 contextual disclosure, not a generic reusable medical consent or another consent engine.

Patient preview/create require current PATIENT identity, owned live session/active clinic and own appointment/source; no client patient/clinic/price/doctor/result override. Appointment must already be CONFIRMED/CHECKED_IN/QUEUED/IN_PROGRESS and not past its existing operational limit. Future confirmed appointment may be explicitly authorized in advance. Assigned clinician must be the same account-linked active published same-clinic doctor with published specialty/current DOCTOR/clinic membership and existing clinical-sign qualification (reuse guidance authority), plus explicit current DOCTOR-role CLINICAL_RECORD_VIEW for actual clinician access. No permission or role grant is installed by this task. Ordinary ADMIN/STAFF, another same-clinic doctor and physician impersonation are denied.

Clinician read is bounded and rechecks live physician session/role/qualification/view capability, exact appointment assignment, active patient/clinic, own unwithdrawn processing and care-sharing permission, original supported immutable source/protocol/hash and current legal policy. Use existing consultation operational bounds for conservative context availability: from startsAt−15minutes; CONFIRMED through endsAt+60minutes, operational states through endsAt+4hours. Cancellation/no-show/completion stops this new care-context channel immediately. These bounds do not change chat/lifecycle or authorize historical medical records; post-visit published notes/treatment/follow-up keep their existing separate contracts. Policy/source/role/qualification/assignment loss suppresses delivery, with no fallback/rebinding or source mutation. Final patient withdrawal remains possible after any dependency retirement and outside the appointment window.

New self API under patients/me/appointments/:appointmentId/assessment-sharing:
- GET preview?submissionId=UUID: exact safe source/assigned doctor/context hash plus purpose-specific processing/care policy bodies+hashes; no consent or share created.
- GET collection: bounded own share history/current state, page1..50; own references only.
- POST collection, Idempotency-Key: submissionId,contextHash,processingConsentRecordId,carePolicyVersionId,carePolicyContentHash,careSharingAccepted:true; validates exact preview again and atomically records retained acceptance and audit.
- POST :shareId/withdrawal, Idempotency-Key: final self-only privacy withdrawal, no purchase refund/answer deletion/clinical mutation.

GET clinical/appointments/:appointmentId/assessment-context requires DOCTOR plus existing CLINICAL_RECORD_VIEW and the resource gates above; bounded page1..50. Return only authorized appointment/patient references and exact source/version/title/completedAt, technical algorithm/hash provenance and minimal educational result-band summary with educationalOnly:true/clinicalFact:false. No raw responses/score, joint/counterpart data, private medical consent/session/actor IDs or clinical endorsement. Query minimal metadata in SQL, reuse existing technical provenance helper/current O02 review/qualified authority; no engine or workspace rebuilt. No inferred diagnosis, diet or automated release/review.

All reads/commands private/no-store, existing self/read/write/clinical permissions and authenticated limiter, atomic metadata-only ClinicalAccessAudit. Failure to audit fails closed and rolls back mutations. SQL validates immutable scope/exact source and purpose/policy/consent/identity/appointment/FKs, final withdrawal and active uniqueness; current access helpers are server-authoritative and not persisted state. Stabilize appointment→source protocol/definition→consents/policies in consistent ordering; clinician reads use a coherent snapshot. No external side effects, frontend, provider, new permission, booking pricing/start/end/clinical-note mutation or program implementation in this task.

Acceptance: real existing paid engine result→patient exact preview/explicit same-appointment consent→assigned clinician bounded summary; ONLINE/IN_PERSON, both private couple beneficiary ownership and legacy exact opt-in; unpaid/incomplete/invalidated/foreign source/partner/clinic/admin/unassigned/unlinked/disabled/session/role/view/qualification denial; no score/answers and no consent on GET; timing/terminal/current source/legal/medical withdrawal gates and no old fallback; retries/concurrency/safe final withdrawal/SQL immutability and audit rollback; fresh/additive populated preservation/no drift, focused existing clinical/booking/chat/individual/couple regressions, official API/client/consumer checks/main review/evidence/independent commit. Actual purpose-specific legal approval, clinical source/content and operational capability enablement remain release gates.
